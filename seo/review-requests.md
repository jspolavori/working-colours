# Working Colours — Review Request Templates

5 templates for asking happy clients to leave a Google review.

---

## 1. WhatsApp Message (≤50 words)

Hi [Name], really glad the job went well. Would mean a lot if you had a moment to leave us a Google review — just a couple of sentences is perfect. Here's the link: [LINK]. Cheers, John

---

## 2. SMS (≤40 words)

Hi [Name], thanks for having us — really glad you're happy with the result. If you get a chance, a quick Google review helps us out a lot: [LINK]. Cheers, John from Working Colours

---

---

## 4. In-Person Script (Verbal — at final walkthrough)

"Really glad you're happy with how it turned out. One quick favour — if you get a chance in the next couple of days, would you mind leaving us a Google review? Just a couple of sentences about your experience. It genuinely helps us out when people are looking for someone they can trust. I'll flick you a message with the link."

*(Follow up with WhatsApp or SMS within 24 hours while the experience is fresh.)*

---

## 5. Follow-Up Reminder (sent 5–7 days after first request if no review left)

Hi [Name], just a quick follow-up — I sent a review link through last week in case it got buried. Here it is again: [LINK]. No worries if you haven't had a chance, just helps us when people can see honest feedback from real customers. Thanks again — John

---

**Notes on timing:**
- Best time to ask: at the final walkthrough, when enthusiasm is highest
- WhatsApp tends to get the highest response rate on the Northern Beaches
- Don't send more than two follow-ups — one initial ask and one reminder maximum
- Replace [LINK] with your Google Business Profile review link
