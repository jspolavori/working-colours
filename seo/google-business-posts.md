### Post 6 — The Water Test for Decks

**Headline:** Your Deck is Telling You Something — Here's How to Listen

**Body:**
The easiest way to check if your deck needs restaining takes 30 seconds.

Pour a small amount of water on the deck surface and watch what happens:

✅ Water beads up → protection is still working. Keep an eye on it.
⚠️ Water soaks in → protection has failed. The timber is absorbing moisture.

If water is soaking in, every rain event is getting into your timber boards. Over time, that causes swelling, cupping, cracking, and eventually structural damage to the boards themselves.

On the Northern Beaches, the combination of UV, salt, and moisture means decks need attention more often than most people expect — typically every one to three years depending on exposure.

An honest assessment is always the first step. We'll tell you exactly where your deck is at.

### Post 8 — Salt Air and Exterior Paint

**Headline:** Salt Air Is Working Against Your Paint Right Now

**Body:**
If you live within a kilometre of the ocean on the Northern Beaches, salt air is constantly depositing on your exterior surfaces.

Left unchecked, salt contamination:
→ Attacks the paint binder and breaks it down over time
→ Causes adhesion failure — paint peeling from below
→ Corrodes any metal fixings under the paint surface

The simplest thing you can do: wash your exterior walls once a year. A low-pressure garden hose and mild detergent removes salt deposits before they work their way into the paint film. Takes an hour. Adds years to the paint job.

When it is time for a repaint, pressure washing to remove all salt contamination is the first and non-negotiable preparation step.

We work on coastal homes every day. We know the environment and we know what holds up in it.

### Post 10 — When to Repaint the Exterior

**Headline:** How Do You Know When It's Time to Repaint the Exterior?

**Body:**
Here are the signs I look for when assessing an exterior:

🔴 Peeling or flaking — paint has lost adhesion. Act now.
🔴 Bubbling — moisture is trapped under the film. Act now.
🟡 Chalking — run your hand across the wall. White powder = the binder is breaking down.
🟡 Cracking — hairline cracks in the paint film mean it has lost flexibility.
🟡 Mould growth — the anti-microbial additives have been exhausted.
🟢 Fading — colour is lighter than when painted. Earlier stage degradation.

If you're seeing red flags, don't wait. Failing exterior paint exposes your substrate to moisture — on the Northern Beaches that means accelerated damage.

If you're seeing yellow flags, start planning rather than reacting.

I'm happy to come out and do an honest assessment of your home. Free, no obligation.

### Post 14 — Deck Staining Service

**Headline:** Deck Staining on the Northern Beaches — Here's How We Do It

**Body:**
A proper deck restoration isn't just slapping a coat of oil on the boards. Here's the process we follow every time:

1️⃣ Pressure wash — remove dirt, mould, and failed product
2️⃣ Sand — open the timber grain, create a consistent surface
3️⃣ Treat any mould — kill and remove before sealing over it
4️⃣ Gap fill — address splits and cracks in boards
5️⃣ First coat — applied evenly, working with the grain
6️⃣ Second coat — after appropriate drying time between coats

Skipping any of these steps means the product either doesn't penetrate properly or fails faster than it should.

On the Northern Beaches, we see decks all the time that were "done" but weren't prepared properly. The oil is sitting on the surface rather than penetrating — and it's already failing.
