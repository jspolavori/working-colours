## Submission notes

- **Consistency is critical.** Use exactly the same business name, address, phone, and website URL on every listing. Inconsistency across directories confuses Google's local algorithm.
- **NAP format:** Working Colours Painting Services | 0434 030 222 | 58-60 Park St, Narrabeen NSW 2101 | https://www.wcpainting.com.au
- **Priority order:** Start with the HIGH priority group and work through systematically before moving to MEDIUM.
- **Claim existing listings:** Before creating new listings, search for existing ones that may have been auto-created. Claim and update rather than creating duplicates.
- **Request reviews:** After listing on review-focused platforms (Word of Mouth, Product Review, Yelp), ask satisfied clients to leave reviews on those platforms.
