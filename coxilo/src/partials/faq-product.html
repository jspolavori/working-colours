What is the Coxilo Contour Pillow?

Confirmed design direction

It's our first pillow: an ivory pillow with a sculpted, butterfly-like silhouette. A shallow central area gives your head somewhere to settle, and the edges rise in soft curves around it. The cover has a fine textured finish.

How do I choose a pillow that suits me?

Think about how you usually fall asleep, the pillow height that feels comfortable to you, and how firm your mattress is. A contoured shape is a matter of personal preference, so it suits some people more than others.
