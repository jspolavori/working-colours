The shape

A gentle hollow, softly held

Look along the pillow from the side and you'll see the idea: the edges rise in a slow curve, then ease down into a shallow centre. It's a calm, sculptural shape that sits neatly in front of your everyday pillows or on its own.

The textured cover catches the light softly, so the pillow looks considered on a made bed and inviting on an unmade one.

Finding your fit

Back, side or stomach sleeper?

The way you usually fall asleep is a good place to start when choosing a pillow. Here's what people in each position often look for.

  • Back sleepers

    Often prefer a pillow of medium height, so the head isn't pushed too far forward or left to tip back.

  • Side sleepers

    Often prefer a higher, fuller pillow that fills the space between the ear and the shoulder.

  • Stomach sleepers

    Often prefer a low, soft pillow, or a very thin one, so the head stays close to the mattress.
