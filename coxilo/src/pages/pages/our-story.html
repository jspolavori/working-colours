Our story

Comfort comes home.

Coxilo is a new sleep brand for Australian bedrooms. We started with a simple belief: the room you end each day in deserves as much care as any other.

{{image:contour-pillow:3:eager}}

Making room to wind down

Most evenings follow a familiar pattern. The kettle, the dishes, one more message, one more episode. Then, finally, the bedroom. It's often the last room we think about and the first one we need at the end of a long day.

We think small changes can make that room feel different. A bed that looks inviting when you walk in. A pillow you're glad to sink into. Fewer things, chosen more carefully.

Fewer things, chosen with care

Rather than filling a catalogue, we're choosing a small number of sleep essentials and taking our time with each one. We look at the shape, the feel of the fabric and how it sits alongside the bedding you already own.

Our first product is the Coxilo Contour Pillow: a sculpted ivory pillow with a gentle hollow at its centre. Pillowcases and other bedroom essentials will follow, but only once we're happy with them.

Straightforward, from the start

We'd rather tell you what we know than fill a page with promises. Where a detail, like a specification or a delivery time, is still being confirmed with our supplier, we say so. When it's confirmed, we'll publish it.
