Privacy

Draft for launch. This describes how the site works today, during the preview. A full privacy policy will be published before checkout opens.

What this site stores

Your shopping bag is saved in your own browser's local storage, on your device, so it's still there when you come back. It isn't sent to us. You can clear it by removing the items from your bag or clearing your browser's site data.

What we don't collect

During the preview there are no accounts, no checkout, no forms and no analytics or advertising trackers on this site. We don't ask for, or receive, your name, address or payment details.

Third-party services

Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, and preview images may be served by our image host. As with any website, these services receive standard request information such as your IP address when your browser loads them.

Before launch

When checkout opens, we'll update this policy to explain what information is collected to process and deliver orders, who it's shared with, and how to contact us about it, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
