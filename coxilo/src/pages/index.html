Design details

Shaped around a quiet idea

Every curve of the contour pillow has a job. Here is the shape we're developing with our supplier.

A sculpted silhouette
Wide, rounded wings give the pillow its butterfly-like outline and a soft, generous presence on the bed.
A central resting area
A shallow dip at the centre gives your head a natural place to settle.
Raised, curved edges
The edges rise gently around the centre, so the pillow holds its shape and outline.
A textured cover
A fine textured finish in warm ivory that pairs easily with linen and cotton bedding.

Final dimensions, fill and firmness are being confirmed with our supplier and will be listed on the product page.
